These are supplementary programmes, extra-curricular offerings, and enrichment activities that do not replace a school but can strengthen one. They are relevant to families who choose a local school (Emil Weder, a microschool, or online provision) and want to fill gaps in the curriculum, broaden their child's subject exposure, or build social connections that the selected schooling arrangement doesn't provide on its own.

A partial option might be an after-school science programme, a weekend arts or coding workshop, a sports league, a mentorship scheme, a holiday intensive, or a structured community service placement. Each profile states what the offering covers, who runs it, what it costs, and which gaps in the ten decision variables it addresses.

## Partial option profiles

**[[content/options/partial/elsenburg|Elsenburg Agricultural Occupational Qualifications]]** — Two fully funded occupational qualifications (Livestock Farmer NQF 3, Orchard and Vineyard Foreman NQF 4) offered by Elsenburg Agricultural Training Institute through workplace-based learning on host farms. Relevant to families weighing post-school pathways in the valley's agricultural economy. The key bottleneck is securing a host farm placement. Applications run 1 April to 30 June annually; learners must have completed Grade 9. All claims IC or MS.

**[[cathsseta-tourist-guide|CATHSSETA Tourist Guide Training]]** — The regulated qualification required to work legally as a tourist guide in South Africa. FET Certificate: Tourist Guiding (SAQA 71549, NQF Level 4). Requires Grade 12. Training costs R6,500 to R18,900 through Cape Town or online providers; provincial registration costs R240. Relevant to the W2BF's first development priority (tourism) and the valley's shortage of registered guides. All claims IC or MS.

## How to read these profiles

Every profile in all three categories follows the same [[tools/template|template]], structured around the ten decision variables. The summary table at the top gives a quick read. The variable-by-variable assessment gives the detail. The "Best fit" section states who the option suits. The "Key unknowns" section lists what still needs verifying.

Evidence standards apply across all three categories. 
- **IC** (Independently Corroborated) means confirmed through independent sources. 
- **MS** (Mission-Sourced) means the claim comes from project documents. 
- **TBV** (To Be Verified) means the claim is plausible but unconfirmed.
