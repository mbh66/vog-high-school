Fully funded one-year occupational qualifications in agriculture, offered by Elsenburg Agricultural Training Institute (Western Cape Department of Agriculture). Available to school leavers from Grade 9 upwards and to working adults with farm experience. Not a school and not a replacement for one, but a post-school or parallel pathway that connects directly to the valley's agricultural economy.

This profile was prompted by information shared at the inaugural Ward 2 Business and Community Development Forum meeting (16 September 2026), where a Department of Agriculture representative briefed the forum on the programme. Most valley residents, including commercial farmers who could serve as host farms, were unaware of it.

## What it is

Elsenburg Agricultural Training Institute offers occupational qualifications through its Agricultural Skills Development (ASD) programme. These are one-year, full-time programmes combining classroom instruction at the Elsenburg campus with practical training on a host farm. The qualifications are accredited by the Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO).

Two occupational qualifications are currently offered:

**Occupational Certificate: Livestock Farmer (Livestock Farm Supervisor)**
NQF Level 3. OFO Number 99253.

Entry requirements: NQF Level 1 (Grade 9) with English and Mathematical Literacy, or equivalent farm experience. Must have access to a farm for practical training.

**Occupational Certificate: Orchard and Vineyard Foreman**
NQF Level 4. OFO Number 110667.

Entry requirements: Five years supervisory experience on a horticultural farm with AET Level 3 literacy and numeracy, or NQF Level 2 (Grade 10) with English and Mathematics Literacy. Must have access to a farm for practical training.

The Department of Agriculture representative at the W2BF meeting indicated that additional specialisations (orchards, wine making, and others) are being added at Level 4. The brochure confirms only the two qualifications listed above as of 2026.

## Structure

The programme runs for one year, structured as alternating blocks: a period at the Elsenburg campus (classroom instruction, computer-based learning, practical demonstrations) followed by a period on a host farm (supervised practical work). The W2BF meeting briefing described this as approximately one month at college, one month on farm, though the exact schedule may vary by programme.

During college weeks, learners stay in hostel accommodation at Elsenburg. During farm weeks, they work on the host farm they secured before application.

Graduation is scheduled for early December annually.

## Cost

The programme is fully funded by the Western Cape Department of Agriculture. The following costs are covered:

- Registration and class fees
- Hostel accommodation at Elsenburg
- Meals during college weeks
- Computer usage, copying, and printing
- Stipend (reported at R60 per day during college weeks at the W2BF meeting; not confirmed in the brochure)
- Protective clothing

The applicant is responsible for transport to and from Elsenburg and to and from the host farm. No other costs are charged.

## The host farm requirement

This is the critical bottleneck for valley families. An applicant must secure a placement on a host farm before submitting their application. The application requires a letter from the farm confirming practical placement.

For Genadendal and Greyton residents, this means either: finding a commercial farm in the valley willing to serve as a host farm, or finding a host farm elsewhere in the Western Cape.

The W2BF meeting identified this as a solvable problem. Several commercial farmers attended the meeting and the skills gap discussion confirmed that valley farms are expanding and will need more skilled labour over the next five years. Farms currently recruit from outside the area to fill positions that local residents could hold with the right qualifications.

The W2BF Action Item 5 (compile a ward-level skills needs list) is the mechanism through which host farm placements could be coordinated. Whether valley farmers will register as host farms for 2028 Elsenburg applicants is TBV through the W2BF.

## Application

Applications open **1 April** and close **30 June** each year. No late applications are accepted.

Applications are submitted through the EATI Online Application System at www.elsenburg.com.

Required documents (from brochure):
- Certified copy of ID
- Certified copy of academic results
- CV
- Proof of residence
- Letter from host farm confirming practical placement

Contact: DOA.Study@westerncape.gov.za / +27 21 808 5451

For the 2028 programme: applications open 1 April 2027, close 30 June 2027. This means the coordination work (identifying host farms, informing potential applicants, assisting with applications) should begin in early 2027 at the latest.

## Why this matters for the high school decision

This profile sits in the partial options section because Elsenburg is not a school. It does not replace the high school decision. But it changes the post-school calculation in two ways.

First, for a learner whose likely pathway is the local agricultural economy (and the W2BF meeting confirmed that this economy is growing), the Elsenburg qualification is a direct, free, one-year route to employment. A learner who completes Grade 9 at Emil Weder or any other school and enters the Livestock Farmer programme at NQF Level 3 emerges with a recognised occupational certificate, practical farm experience, and a direct connection to employers. The high school does not need to do everything for this learner. It needs to get them to Grade 9 with adequate English and numeracy, and it needs to tell them this programme exists.

Second, for a learner at any school who takes Agricultural Sciences as a CAPS subject, the Elsenburg practical blocks are a way to deepen the subject beyond the classroom. This is particularly relevant if the proposed Valley of Grace Learning Campus (see [[options/long-term/learning-campus|long-term options]]) integrates Elsenburg partnership into its programme design. Even before that campus exists, a motivated learner or family can pursue this pathway independently.

The programme also serves adults. A farm worker with five years of experience and AET Level 3 literacy can enter the Orchard and Vineyard Foreman qualification at NQF Level 4. This is relevant to the W2BF's skills development priority and to the CET satellite proposal in the Learning Campus model: an adult who completes GETC (Grade 9 equivalent) through the CET programme becomes eligible for the Livestock Farmer qualification.

## Best fit

This programme suits school leavers (from Grade 9 upwards) who are interested in agricultural work and want a practical, funded qualification. It suits adults already working on farms who want to formalise their skills and advance to supervisory roles. It suits families whose children are not on a university track and who want a concrete post-school pathway that connects to the local economy.

It does not suit learners who want a university pathway (the qualification is occupational, not academic). It does not suit learners with no interest in agriculture. It requires the applicant to find a host farm independently, which is a real barrier for community members without existing connections to commercial farmers.

## Key unknowns

- Whether valley commercial farmers would register as host farms for the 2028 intake. **TBV** through the W2BF working group and Action Item 5.
- The exact stipend amount. The W2BF briefing stated R60/day during college weeks. The brochure confirms a stipend is provided but does not state the amount. **TBV** with Elsenburg directly.
- Whether Elsenburg would deliver short courses or practical blocks at a location closer to the valley (they already run decentralised training at Bredasdorp, approximately 60 km from Genadendal). **TBV** with Elsenburg.
- Whether additional NQF Level 4 specialisations (orchards, wine making) will be available for the 2028 intake. **TBV** with Elsenburg.
- How many valley residents would apply if they knew the programme existed and had host farm access. No demand data exists. **TBV** through community outreach via the W2BF and the Transformasie Komitee.
