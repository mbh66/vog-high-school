## Why this profile exists

Most English-speaking Greyton parents will not consider Hoërskool Overberg because of the language barrier. This profile exists for two reasons. First, some valley families are Afrikaans-speaking and for them this school is a strong option. Second, the school's boarding facilities ("Koshuise") make it the only confirmed boarding option in the immediate region. If Overberg High School (English-medium) does not offer boarding, Hoërskool Overberg's hostels are the nearest residential schooling option for valley families.

## Variable-by-variable assessment

### 1. Language of instruction

Afrikaans. The school's Instagram presence and website are entirely in Afrikaans. For Afrikaans-speaking families in Genadendal, the Farm 39 communities, and Heuwelkroon, this is not a barrier. For English-speaking Greyton families, it carries the same limitation as Emil Weder, with the added cost of transport or boarding.

### 2. Daily logistics

Same distance as Overberg High School (35 km from Greyton). The difference is that boarding is confirmed. The school's website references "Koshuise" (hostels) for boys and girls.

Weekly boarding eliminates the daily commute. The child stays at the hostel Monday to Friday and comes home on weekends. This removes the lift club dependency and the daily road risk, at the cost of family separation during the week.

### 3. School type and registration

Public school, WCED-registered, SGB-governed. Same institutional framework as Emil Weder and Overberg High.

### 4. Curriculum and examination body

CAPS, DBE examination. Standard NSC. No ambiguity.

### 5. Cost

School fees (Quintile classification TBV) plus boarding fees. Government school hostels in rural Western Cape towns typically charge R20,000 to R40,000/year for boarding. Add school fees and incidentals:

**Estimated annual cost:** R30,000 to R70,000, depending on fee and boarding levels.

**Five-year cost:** R150,000 to R350,000.

This is comparable to the Overberg High School lift-club scenario and cheaper than solo driving to Caledon.

### 6. Pedagogy and values

Standard academic instruction. As a larger school than Emil Weder, Hoërskool Overberg likely has more specialist teachers, better facilities, and a wider extracurricular programme. The school's Instagram shows active sport (rugby, netball, athletics) and cultural activities.

### 7. Subject availability

Larger school, so a wider subject menu is expected. Specific list TBV.

### 8. Post-school pathway

Standard NSC. Boland TVET Caledon campus is in the same town.

### 9. Social and community considerations

A boarding child joins the hostel community. This can be a strong social experience: close friendships, shared routines, mentoring by older learners. It can also be difficult, depending on the child's maturity and the hostel culture.

The child's primary peer group becomes the school, not the valley. Weekend connections to Greyton or Genadendal persist but are compressed.

For Afrikaans-speaking families, this school offers a natural linguistic and cultural fit with the additional structure and social experience of boarding.

### 10. Silverwood-specific

Not applicable.

## Best fit

Hoërskool Overberg suits families where: the child is Afrikaans-speaking; the family wants the structure and social environment of boarding; the budget can sustain R30,000 to R70,000/year; the child is mature enough for weekly separation from home.

It may also suit English-speaking families who: have a child with strong Afrikaans; value the boarding experience and want to avoid daily commuting; cannot afford independent boarding further afield.
