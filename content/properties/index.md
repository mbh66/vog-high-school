Here we provide current listings of properties that may be suitable for a school in Greyton. No further assessment has been done on any of the properties listed here.

- [13 Park Street](https://www.property24.com/for-sale/greyton/caledon/western-cape/7874/117166863), R2.25m - located far from any surrounding houses, which solves any potential concerns about noise.
- [Uitkyk Hub](https://ecolodgegreyton.co.za) - the current site of Silverwood Primary, but managed by Uitkyk Primary, on the adjacent property. Potential complications with negotiating a long-term lease.

## Possible considerations

The feasibility of these properties is highly unlikely, but they are listed here purely to show what underutilised assets the valley currently offers.

- **Greyton Commonage** (erf 595) - could only be considered as part of a wider eco-education initiative; potentially earmarked for expansion of the Greyton Nature Reserve
