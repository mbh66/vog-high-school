**Michael Mount Waldorf School (Johannesburg)**
- https://www.doris.school/schools/south-africa/michael-mount-waldorf-school — Doris.school profile. Day school, Bryanston. IEB NSC matric. Waldorf curriculum to Class 11, matric through IEB. Referenced in Variables 4, 6.

**Michael Oak Waldorf School (Cape Town)**

**Roseway Waldorf School (KwaZulu-Natal)**

## Examination Bodies and Curriculum

**SACAI (South African Comprehensive Assessment Institute)**
- https://www.sacai.org.za/ — Official website. FAQ confirms Umalusi matric certificates accepted by all SA universities. Explains registration for distance education providers, SBA requirements, examination centre arrangements. Referenced in Variables 3, 4.

**CambriLearn: CAPS Curriculum Online School Guide**
- https://cambrilearn.com/blog/caps-curriculum-online-school-south-africa — Comprehensive guide to CAPS online. Confirms DBE, IEB, SACAI NSC equivalence. Explains FET subject structure (4 compulsory + 3 electives), promotion requirements, switching between examination bodies. Referenced in Variables 3, 4, 7.

**CambriLearn: Online School University Acceptance**
- https://cambrilearn.com/blog/online-school-university-acceptance-south-africa — Confirms university admission treats SACAI, IEB, and DBE results identically. APS calculated the same way. IEB 2024: 98.47% pass rate, 89.37% bachelor passes. Referenced in Variable 4.

**CambriLearn: What Is SACAI? A Parent's Guide**
- https://cambrilearn.com/blog/what-is-sacai-a-parents-guide — Explains SACAI's role, Umalusi accreditation, examination centre requirements. Cites Umalusi's January 2025 advisory confirming universities cannot differentiate between DBE, IEB, and SACAI NSC certificates. Referenced in Variable 4.

**CambriLearn: SACAI Registered Schools Guide**
- https://cambrilearn.com/blog/sacai-registered-online-schools-matric-pathway-guide — Detailed guide on the SACAI matric pathway for online learners. Confirms SACAI results fully accepted at all SA universities. Explains difference between SACAI, IEB, and DBE pathways. Referenced in Variables 3, 4.

**CambriLearn: Grade 10 Subject Choices Complete Guide**
- https://cambrilearn.com/blog/grade-10-subject-choices-south-africa-complete-guide — Explains CAPS FET subject structure. Four compulsory subjects plus three electives. Covers IEB and Cambridge subject selection. Notes that switching subjects after Grade 10 is difficult. Referenced in Variable 7.

**Teneo Online School: SACAI Accreditation Explained**
- https://www.teneoschool.co.za/blog/sacai-accreditation-explained-what-parents-need-to-know — Confirms SACAI NSC = Umalusi matric certificate, accepted by all SA universities. Explains that final matric exams are written in person at invigilated centres. Referenced in Variable 4.

**Homeschool Start Guide: Homeschooling South Africa Exams**
- https://homeschoolstartguide.com/blog/homeschooling-south-africa-exams — Explains SACAI, IEB, and Cambridge pathways for home learners. Key distinction: SACAI/IEB issue the NSC directly; Cambridge requires a separate USAf Matriculation Exemption. Referenced in Variable 4.

**Jozi Kids: Local NSC or International Matric Pass**
- https://www.jozikids.co.za/blog/education/nsc-other-education-system/ — Overview of DBE, IEB, SACAI, and international curriculum options. Confirms international study possible with any NSC. Referenced in Variable 4.

**Matric.co.za: Difference Between IEB and NSC**
- https://www.matric.co.za/what-is-the-difference-between-ieb-and-nsc/ — Explains IEB vs DBE NSC. Both registered by SAQA. Both accepted for international university applications. Referenced in Variable 4.

## TVET and Post-School

**Boland TVET College**
- https://fundiconnect.co.za/institutions/boland-tvet-college — Fundiconnect profile. Five campuses: Stellenbosch, Paarl, Worcester, Strand, Caledon. Caledon campus serves agricultural and small business sectors. Referenced in Variable 8.
- https://fundiconnect.co.za/?p=70308 — Additional Fundiconnect entry. Confirms rural Boland focus, Caledon campus opened 2002.
- https://briefly.co.za/50613-boland-college-online-application-forms-courses-fees-requirements.html — Briefly.co.za overview. Confirms five campuses, public TVET institution, serves Helderberg, Breede River Valley, Overberg, Winelands, Hex River Valley. Referenced in Variable 8.
- https://varsitywise.com/posts/boland-tvet-college-opens-2026-applications-your-g — Varsitywise 2026 applications guide. Programmes: Economic and Management Sciences, Engineering, Hospitality and Tourism, IT and Agriculture. Referenced in Variable 8.
- https://varsitywise.com/posts/boland-college-programmes-available-for-2026-semester-2 — Caledon campus Semester 2 2026 programmes: Office Administrator (L5), Management Assistant (L5), Tourism Information Officer (L5), ECD Practitioner (L4). Referenced in Variable 8.
- https://varsitywise.com/posts/boland-college-semester-2-applications-open — Semester 2 2026 application dates (4 to 29 May 2026). Online application only.

**Western Cape Government: TVET Overview**
- https://www.westerncape.gov.za/your_gov/70/documents/public_info/T/42697 — Provincial TVET overview. Accepts students from Grade 9 upwards. Fields: engineering, ICT, hospitality, food service, tourism, haircare, ECD, management. Referenced in Variable 8.

**IOL: TVET Colleges vs Universities**
- https://iol.co.za/news/education/2026-01-08-tvet-colleges-vs-universities-why-skills-may-matter-more-than-degrees/ — January 2026 article. NSFAS funds TVET students.
