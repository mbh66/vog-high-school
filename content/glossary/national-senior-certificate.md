The standard South African school-leaving qualification, awarded on completion of Grade 12. The NSC is administered by one of three [[umalusi|Umalusi]]-accredited examination bodies: the [[dbe|DBE]] (public schools and some independents), the [[ieb|IEB]] (most high-fee independents), and [[sacai|SACAI]] (most online and distance providers). Umalusi has stated directly that universities cannot differentiate between the three when presented with the NSC certificate.

Admission decisions rest on the qualification level ([[bachelor-pass|Bachelor pass]], Diploma, or Higher Certificate) and the subject marks, which convert to the same [[admission-point-score|APS]] scale regardless of examination body.
