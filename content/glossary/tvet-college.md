Technical and Vocational Education and Training college. TVET colleges accept students from Grade 9 onwards. They deliver [[ncv|National Certificate Vocational]] qualifications (Levels 2 to 4) and [[nated|NATED]] diplomas (N4 to N6) in fields including engineering, hospitality and tourism, business, IT, agriculture, and early childhood development. TVETs fall under [[dhet|DHET]] and are funded by NSFAS for qualifying students.

[[boland-tvet-college|Boland TVET College]]'s Caledon campus is the nearest option for valley learners, roughly 35 km from Greyton.
