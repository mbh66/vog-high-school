The [[seta|SETA]] covering agriculture. AgriSETA accredits training providers and funds learnerships in agricultural qualifications. The [[glossary/elsenburg|Elsenburg]] Agricultural Training Institute's occupational qualifications sit within AgriSETA's remit and are accredited by the [[qcto|QCTO]].
