The statutory quality council for general and further education and training in South Africa. Umalusi accredits the three [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] examination bodies ([[dbe|DBE]], [[ieb|IEB]], and [[sacai|SACAI]]) and issues advisories confirming their equivalent standing. A provider not registered with a Umalusi-accredited examination body cannot issue the NSC.
