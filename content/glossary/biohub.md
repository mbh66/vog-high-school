The proposed integrated community, education, and economic hub for the [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]]. The BioHub Founding Brief describes a Learning Hub (of which the proposed [[silverwood-school|Silverwood School]] forms a part), a research and heritage component, and integration with the [[goe|GoE]] [[bioscore|BioScore]] and [[nature-realm|Nature Realm]] platforms. The BioHub is one of the anchor concepts that gives the valley's education proposals their broader institutional context.
