A public TVET college with a campus in Caledon, approximately 35 km from Greyton. The Caledon campus offers Office Administration, Management, Tourism, and Early Childhood Development programmes; Engineering and agricultural technology are delivered at the Worcester and Strand campuses.

A learner completing Grade 9 at any school in the valley can enter a Boland [[ncv|NCV]] Level 2 programme; a Grade 12 learner enters NATED programmes at N4. NSFAS funding is available for qualifying students.
