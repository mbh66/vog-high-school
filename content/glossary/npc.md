A Non-Profit Company registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) under the Companies Act. The NPC is the legal vehicle proposed for the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]]: it would hold the school registration under [[sasa|SASA]] Section 46, the [[qcto|QCTO]] Skills Development Provider accreditation, the [[cet-college|CET]] satellite agreement, and any leases or property arrangements. Registration typically takes 2 to 3 months.

Registration as an NPO (Department of Social Development) and a PBO (SARS) follows the NPC and unlocks Section 18A tax-deductible donations.
