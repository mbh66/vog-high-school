The national curriculum of South Africa, mandated by the [[dbe|Department of Basic Education]]. CAPS specifies content, sequencing, and assessment weighting for every subject from Grade R to Grade 12. Every public school follows it. Most independent schools follow it, sometimes with enrichment, and every accredited CAPS-aligned online provider follows it. A child following CAPS in any setting covers the same material; the differences between schools lie in how well they teach it, how much they supplement it, and which [[national-senior-certificate|examination body]] administers the final assessment.

CAPS is the path of least friction for South African post-school pathways. Every SA university, [[tvet-college|TVET college]], and employer recognises the qualification it leads to without extra steps. The alternative to CAPS in this Decision Tool is [[cambridge-international|Cambridge International]].
