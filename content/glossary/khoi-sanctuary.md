The Khoi settlement and sanctuary landscape at [[genadendal|Genadendal]] predating the [[moravian-mission|Moravian mission]] of 1738. The Khoi heritage is a subject for History and heritage guiding curriculum in the [[bioregional-learning-centre|BLC]] and [[cathsseta|CATHSSETA]]-accredited tourist guide training, and one of the Tribes (T) layer references in the [[time-framework|TIME framework]] curriculum.
