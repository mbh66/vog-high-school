The dedicated matric preparation year run by South African [[waldorf-education|Waldorf schools]] following the completion of their own 12-year developmental curriculum in Class 12. Class 13 aligns to the [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] and is written through whichever examination body the school is registered with ([[dbe|DBE]], [[ieb|IEB]], or [[sacai|SACAI]]).

Class 13 means the child is in school for 13 years, not 12. Some Waldorf schools now integrate [[caps|CAPS]] content earlier to avoid the extra year. Michael Oak in Cape Town includes a Class 13 matric year; Constantia Waldorf structures its programme to align with CAPS matriculation earlier.
