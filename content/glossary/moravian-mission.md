The oldest Moravian mission station in southern Africa, founded at [[genadendal|Genadendal]] in 1738. The mission governed land, labour, and trade through church authority for over 250 years, an example of the Institutions (I) layer in the [[time-framework|TIME framework]]. Its 2038 tercentenary is a fixed strategic milestone for [[ward-2|Ward 2]] planning and shapes the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]] and [[bioregional-learning-centre|BLC]] programming.

The mission sits on Moravian Church land within the Genadendal settlement, alongside the Genadendal Mission Museum, the historic church, and the watermill.
