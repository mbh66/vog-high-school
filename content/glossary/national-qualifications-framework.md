The framework administered by [[saqa|SAQA]] that places every recognised qualification in South Africa on a ten-level scale. Grade 9 sits at NQF Level 1. The [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] sits at NQF Level 4, alongside occupational certificates such as [[cathsseta|CATHSSETA]]'s FET Certificate: Tourist Guiding and [[glossary/elsenburg|Elsenburg]]'s Orchard and Vineyard Foreman certificate. A bachelor's degree sits at NQF Level 7.

NQF levels matter for post-school entry: a learner completing Grade 9 (NQF 1) qualifies for Elsenburg's Livestock Farmer (NQF 3) programme; a matriculant (NQF 4) qualifies for [[tvet-college|TVET]] NATED N4 diplomas and university admission.
