[[trancraa|TRANCRAA]] land within the Genadendal community with unresolved tenure. Farm 39 recurs in Decision Tool discussion of land, governance, and possible campus sites, and is a Corridor 3 anchor for the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]]. Any use of Farm 39 for education is inseparable from the community's [[communal-property-association|CPA]] process and the [[transformasie-komitee|Transformasie Komitee]].
