An educational orientation that grounds curriculum in the ecology, heritage, and economy of the learner's own place. Geography becomes the local watershed; History becomes the local settlement; Life Sciences becomes local ecology. The [[ward-2-schools-program|Ward 2 Schools Program]] and the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]] are both built on this orientation. The [[content/glossary/treverton-college|Treverton College]] precedent in KwaZulu-Natal demonstrates that place-based programmes become durable when the campus is itself a landscape.
