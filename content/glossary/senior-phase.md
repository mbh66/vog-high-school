The three years bridging primary and [[fet-phase|FET]] schooling under [[caps|CAPS]]: Grades 7, 8, and 9. In high school terms it refers to Grades 8 and 9. Compulsory subjects include two languages, Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Technology, Economic and Management Sciences, Life Orientation, and Creative Arts.

Grade 9 is the earliest exit point for entry into [[tvet-college|TVET colleges]] and the [[content/glossary/elsenburg|Elsenburg]] Livestock Farmer qualification ([[national-qualifications-framework|NQF]] Level 3). [[content/glossary/greyton-house|Greyton House]]'s existing WCED registration covers this phase.
