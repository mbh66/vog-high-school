The provincial education department responsible for schools in the Western Cape. The WCED registers public schools, registers independent schools under [[sasa|SASA]] Section 46, employs public-school teachers, sets quintile classifications, and oversees home-education registration for parents.

The WCED cannot override a public school's language policy: under SASA Section 6, that authority sits with the [[sgb|School Governing Body]]. In Ward 2, WCED oversight applies to [[emil-weder-secondary|Emil Weder Secondary]] and would apply to [[silverwood-school|Silverwood]] once registered.
