The Genadendal community body carrying the mandate for local governance and community-directed decisions in Genadendal. Any proposal for education, land use, or economic activity in the community requires the Komitee's involvement. The [[ward-2-dev-forum|W2DF]] committed at its inaugural meeting to share proposals with the Komitee in writing before seeking endorsement. That protocol structures the proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]] work.
