An accredited online high school registered with [[sacai|SACAI]], operating live classes with trained teachers and support advisors. Received over 19,000 applications for its 2026 intake, indicating mainstream acceptance of online schooling at the secondary level. Approximate fee: R36,000 per year (2026).
