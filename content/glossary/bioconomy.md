The framework, wiki, and practice project describing coordination through networks of mutual commitment: reciprocity traditions older than money, revived through contemporary practice such as commitment pooling, community exchange systems, and commons-based governance. In the Learning Campus curriculum, the BioConomy is the +E (Emergent) layer taught alongside the +M (Market) layer of standard [[caps|CAPS]] Economics and Business Studies.
