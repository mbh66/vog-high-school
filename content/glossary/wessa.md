The national environmental education and conservation society. WESSA operates the Eco-School programme and awarded [[glossary/treverton-college|Treverton College]] its Corporate Award in 2012 for the Wildlife Area. WESSA [[eco-school|Eco-School]] certification is a Phase 1 target for the proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]].
