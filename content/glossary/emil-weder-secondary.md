The only operational high school in the Valley of Grace, located in [[genadendal|Genadendal]] and approximately 7 km from Greyton. Public, [[wced|WCED]]-registered, Afrikaans-medium, following [[caps|CAPS]] and writing the [[dbe|DBE]] [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]]. The lowest-cost option available to valley families and the most diverse peer group; the language barrier is the trade-off for English-speaking families.
