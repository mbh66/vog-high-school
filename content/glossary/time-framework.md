David Ronfeldt's framework for the coordination forms human societies have developed, extending his earlier TIMN model. The four forms are:

- **Tribes (T)**: kinship and mutual obligation.
- **Institutions (I)**: formalised trust, rules that hold between strangers (the [[moravian-mission|Moravian mission]] at Genadendal is one).
- **Markets (M)**: competitive exchange through money and prices (the +M layer taught by [[caps|CAPS]] Economics).
- **Emergent (E)**: networks of mutual commitment operating at scale, drawing on Tribal reciprocity but using formal protocols to move beyond personal memory and reputation (the [[bioconomy|BioConomy]] layer).

Introduced in the [[learning-campus-economics|Learning Campus economics module]]. The valley is treated as a place where all four layers are visible in a single geography.
