The legal entity through which a community holds and administers land under [[trancraa|TRANCRAA]] and the Communal Property Associations Act. A CPA has members, a constitution, and a committee. For Genadendal, the CPA is one of the governance structures through which decisions about [[farm-39|Farm 39]] and other community land would flow.
