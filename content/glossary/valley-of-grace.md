The bioregion covering [[greyton|Greyton]], [[genadendal|Genadendal]], [[bereaville|Bereaville]], [[voorstekraal|Voorstekraal]], and surrounding farms in [[ward-2|Ward 2]] of the [[twkm|Theewaterskloof Municipality]], Western Cape. Approximately 10,000 hectares. The name reflects both the [[moravian-mission|Moravian mission]] heritage of Genadendal (Dutch/Afrikaans for "valley of grace") and the wider community identity that has re-emerged around bioregional regeneration, tourism, agriculture, and heritage. The 2038 tercentenary of the Genadendal mission is a fixed strategic milestone.
