The Supreme Court of Appeal judgment that confirmed a provincial education department may not direct a public school to change its language of instruction. The case established that language policy sits with the [[sgb|School Governing Body]] under [[sasa|SASA]] Section 6, and that the [[wced|WCED]] cannot override it. This is the legal reason a parent cannot compel [[emil-weder-secondary|Emil Weder]] to offer English-medium classes if the SGB has set Afrikaans as the medium.
