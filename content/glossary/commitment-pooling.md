An operational mechanism for [[bioconomy|BioConomy]] coordination drawn from Will Ruddick's work at the Grassroots Economics Foundation. A pool takes contributions of commitments (labour, skills, materials, mentorship) from its members, who can then draw on commitments from others. It is governed through four protocol functions:

- **Curation**: what belongs in the pool.
- **Valuation**: what each commitment is worth relative to others.
- **Limitation**: how much any member can contribute or withdraw.
- **Exchange**: how commitments circulate.

In the proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]] a commitment pool would allow families unable to pay full school fees in rand to contribute through the pool instead, without treating that as charity or a discount.
