The [[seta|SETA]] covering the tourism, hospitality, sport, arts, culture, and conservation sectors. CATHSSETA accredits training providers, funds learnerships, and awards the [[occupational-certificate|FET Certificate: Tourist Guiding]] (SAQA ID 71549, NQF Level 4), the qualification required to work legally as a tourist guide in South Africa.

The proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] would seek CATHSSETA accreditation to deliver tourist guide training in the valley.
