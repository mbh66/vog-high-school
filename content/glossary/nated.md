The classical technical college programme sequence at [[tvet-college|TVET colleges]], running from N1 to N6. N4 requires a matric-level entry ([[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] or equivalent). N6 combined with 18 to 24 months of workplace experience leads to a National Diploma.
