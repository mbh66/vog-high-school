A proposed English-medium, [[caps|CAPS]]-compliant, [[waldorf-education|Waldorf]]-inspired independent high school in the valley, integrated with the [[biohub|BioHub]]. Described in project documents as running [[project-based-learning|project-based learning]] alongside the [[bioscore|BioScore]] and [[nature-realm|Nature Realm]] platforms. Not yet constituted as of September 2026; every claim about Silverwood is [[ms|MS]] and requires direct confirmation with the founders.

Variable 10 of the Decision Tool consolidates the 18 questions parents should ask before treating Silverwood as a real option for 2028.
