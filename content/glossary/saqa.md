The statutory body that oversees the [[national-qualifications-framework|National Qualifications Framework]] and maintains the register of recognised qualifications and unit standards. Every recognised qualification carries a SAQA ID. The [[cathsseta|FET Certificate: Tourist Guiding]] is SAQA ID 71549 at NQF Level 4.
