[[capenature|CapeNature]]'s statutory management plan for a protected area. The Riviersonderend Complex PAMP names education and awareness as management objectives, which provides a formal partnership hook for the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]]. Formal recognition of the BLC as a PAMP partner is a Phase 3 target for the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]].
