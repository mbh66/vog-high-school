Schooling delivered through a digital platform, with the learner working from home or any location with internet access. Providers register with an examination body ([[sacai|SACAI]], [[ieb|IEB]], or Cambridge) as distance education providers under that body's accreditation regime. The examination body, not the WCED, is the primary quality-assurance mechanism.

Providers range from high-structure ([[uct-online-high-school|UCT Online]], [[teneo|Teneo]], Wingu Academy) with timetabled live classes to low-structure (Syllabis, Impaq) with self-paced materials. Fees run from under R6,000 per year to about R45,000 per year, before device, data, and supervision costs.
