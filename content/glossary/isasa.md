The membership association for registered independent schools across Southern Africa. ISASA membership is a marker of an independent school's institutional standing and adherence to professional norms. [[glossary/greyton-house|Greyton House]] is an ISASA member; most established boarding schools in the Western Cape (Bridge House, Somerset College, Bishops) are ISASA-affiliated.
