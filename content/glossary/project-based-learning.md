A pedagogy that organises curriculum around sustained projects that span multiple weeks. Learners work on a real-world problem over days or weeks, drawing on multiple subjects, and are assessed through the project output: a report, a presentation, a built artefact.

The Overberg [[biohub|BioHub]] Founding Brief describes [[silverwood-school|Silverwood]]'s intended pedagogy as "project-based learning integrated with the BioHub's [[bioscore|BioScore]] and [[nature-realm|Nature Realm]] platforms." In that model a Geography project might involve mapping a section of the [[riviersonderend|Riviersonderend]] watershed while meeting CAPS content requirements.
