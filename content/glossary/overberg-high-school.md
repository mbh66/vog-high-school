English-medium public high school in Caledon, approximately 35 km from Greyton. Around 492 learners. [[quintile|Quintile 5]] (fee-paying). Follows [[caps|CAPS]], writes the [[dbe|DBE]] [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]]. The nearest public school offering English-medium instruction. A separate institution from [[hoerskool-overberg|Hoërskool Overberg]], which is Afrikaans-medium and shares Caledon.

Access from Greyton depends on daily transport (lift club, solo drive, scholar service) or weekly boarding. Transport, not fees, dominates the annual cost.
