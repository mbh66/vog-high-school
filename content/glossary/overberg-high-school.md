Dual-medium public high school in Caledon, with English and Afrikaans streams [MS], approximately 35 km from Greyton. Its Afrikaans name is [[hoerskool-overberg|Hoërskool Overberg]]; the two names refer to one school [MS]. The school has hostels ("Koshuise") [MS]. Around 492 learners. [[quintile|Quintile 5]] (fee-paying). Follows [[caps|CAPS]], writes the [[dbe|DBE]] [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]]. The nearest public school offering English-medium instruction.

Access from Greyton depends on daily transport (lift club, solo drive, scholar service) or weekly boarding. Transport, not fees, dominates the annual cost.
