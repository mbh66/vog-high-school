A biodiversity and ecological monitoring platform operated by the [[goe|GoE]] network. Learner fieldwork through iNaturalist and other citizen-science tools generates real BioScore data for the Valley of Grace Realm. In the [[silverwood-school|Silverwood]] proposal and the [[bioregional-learning-centre|BLC]] design, BioScore contributions form part of the learner's assessment record.
