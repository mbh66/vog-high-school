Village in the [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]], Western Cape. Older, predominantly English-speaking, and relatively affluent permanent population. Approximately 7 km from [[genadendal|Genadendal]] and 35 km from Caledon via the R406. The village is the primary source of the English-medium high school demand that drives the Decision Tool.
