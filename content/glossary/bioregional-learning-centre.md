A place-based education and research facility proposed as part of the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]]. The BLC is designed as a node within the ~10,000-hectare Valley of Grace Realm, with defined learning corridors extending into the [[erf-595|Greyton commonage]], the Greyton Nature Reserve, [[farm-39|Farm 39]], and the [[riviersonderend|Riviersonderend]] watershed. Learners take stewardship responsibility for allocated restoration blocks (wetland rehabilitation, alien clearing, erosion management, species monitoring); the conservation area's condition becomes part of their learning record.

International reference points include the Schumacher College Small School (UK), the Findhorn Foundation (Scotland), SEKEM (Egypt), and the Sustainability Institute (Stellenbosch).
