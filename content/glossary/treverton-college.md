An independent boarding school on a 270-hectare estate in Mooi River, KwaZulu-Natal, that hosted the 1982 founding conference of [[eeasa|EEASA]] and spent the following four decades building layered environmental and place-based education programmes. Treverton's Wildlife Area, Outdoor Education programme, Stewardship Programme, Wacky Wednesdays, and Unplugged Days serve as precedents for the proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] and [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]].

The Treverton research brief in the Decision Tool draws out seven transferable lessons for the valley.
