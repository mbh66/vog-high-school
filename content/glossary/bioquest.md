A biannual (April and October) citizen-science cycle within the [[goe|GoE]] network in which learners and community members contribute biodiversity observations and heritage evidence to the [[nature-realm|Valley of Grace Realm]]. Named as an anchor for the [[ward-2-schools-program|Ward 2 Schools Program]]'s inter-school contest.
