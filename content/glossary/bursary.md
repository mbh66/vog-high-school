Financial assistance that reduces or replaces school fees. **Fee exemptions** at public schools are governed by the [[dbe|DBE]]: [[quintile|Quintile]] 4 and 5 public schools must exempt families that cannot afford fees, on application, using the national scale. **Bursaries** at independent and boarding schools are voluntary and set by the school; some are means-tested, some merit-based (academic, sport, music). Applications typically open a year ahead of entry: a child entering Grade 8 in 2028 would apply for bursaries in early to mid-2027.

At public universities and [[tvet-college|TVET colleges]], NSFAS is the national bursary scheme for qualifying students.
