An international curriculum and examination pathway offered by some independent schools and online providers (including [[cambrilearn|CambriLearn]]). It runs through IGCSEs (equivalent to Grade 10) followed by AS and A Levels (equivalent to Grades 11 and 12). Cambridge qualifications are internationally recognised and accepted by South African universities, subject to a separate [[matriculation-exemption|Matriculation Exemption]] application.

Cambridge suits families planning international university applications, or wanting a curriculum that emphasises depth over breadth. It costs more to deliver and examine, and the subject menu is often narrower than at a large [[caps|CAPS]] school.
