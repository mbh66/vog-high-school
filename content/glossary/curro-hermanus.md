An independent high school in Sandbaai, Hermanus, roughly 80 km from Greyton. Part of the Curro Group. Dual-medium (English and Afrikaans). Writes the [[ieb|IEB]] [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]]. Maximum 25 learners per class. A school bus collects from Caledon (35 km from Greyton). Approximate 2026 Grade 12 fee: R88,000 per year.
