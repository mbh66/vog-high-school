The local government ward covering [[greyton|Greyton]], [[genadendal|Genadendal]], [[bereaville|Bereaville]], [[voorstekraal|Voorstekraal]], and surrounding farms. The convening geography for the [[ward-2-schools-program|Ward 2 Schools Program]], the [[ward-2-dev-forum|Ward 2 Business Forum]], and the wider [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]] planning work. Youth unemployment in the 15-to-34 age group exceeds 44%.
