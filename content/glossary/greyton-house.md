English-medium [[isasa|ISASA]]-member independent primary school in Greyton, currently operating Grade R to Grade 7. Its [[wced|WCED]] registration already covers Grades 8 and 9; whether the school will actually offer those grades depends on board clearance and accommodation. If it does, it becomes an in-valley English-medium option for the [[senior-phase|Senior Phase]], with a required transfer for [[fet-phase|FET]] (Grades 10 to 12).
