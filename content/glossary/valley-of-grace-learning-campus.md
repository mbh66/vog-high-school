An integrated institution combining an English-medium independent high school, the [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]], and a community skills centre under a single [[npc|NPC]] governed to represent the whole ward. Earliest high school enrolment: 2029 or 2030; skills centre and adult education components can begin earlier. The campus model responds to the fact that a standalone English-medium high school is not viable in the valley on its own.
