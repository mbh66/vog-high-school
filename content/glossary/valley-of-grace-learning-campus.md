A proposed learning campus for Ward 2 with four parts on shared ground: an English-medium independent boarding school as anchor tenant, a public school of skills, a vocational training centre focused on tourism and conservation, and the [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]] as its tertiary layer. A community trust or [[npc|NPC]] representing the whole ward holds the land and governs the campus. The boarding school leases its site from the trust, and its rent and per-learner levy fund much of the campus's development, including bursary places for valley learners. [[emil-weder-secondary|Emil Weder]] remains the valley's Afrikaans public high school and is invited to use the campus as a partner.

The model responds to the fact that a standalone English-medium day school is not viable in the valley: a boarding school recruits from outside the valley, so its size does not depend on how many children live here. It draws on two precedents: Treverton College in KwaZulu-Natal and Jakes Gerwel Technical School in Bonnievale. Earliest high school enrolment: 2030 (TBV). The vocational training centre and adult education can begin earlier. All claims are MS or TBV.
