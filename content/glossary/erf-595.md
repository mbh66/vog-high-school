The Greyton municipal commonage: approximately 2,200 hectares including the [[greyton|Greyton]] Nature Reserve and surrounding grazing and conservation land. Governed by [[twkm|Theewaterskloof Municipality]] under a management framework that is not fully settled. A contested R100 sale of about 8 hectares on Erf 595 to a private buyer (documented in the Greyton Commonage Research Report) remains unresolved.

Erf 595 is one of the site scenarios considered for [[silverwood-school|Silverwood]] and the proposed [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]].
