The [[wessa|WESSA]]-administered school certification programme that recognises environmental education and stewardship activity within a school. [[content/glossary/treverton-college|Treverton College]] holds Eco-School status alongside its Wildlife Area work. Eco-School certification for participating schools is a Phase 1 target for the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] and one of the "external recognition ratchets" identified in the Treverton research brief.
