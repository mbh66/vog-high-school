The river and mountain catchment defining the northern edge of the [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]]. The Riviersonderend Mountain Catchment Area covers approximately 10,000 hectares and is managed under [[capenature|CapeNature]]'s Riviersonderend Complex Protected Area Management Plan ([[pamp|PAMP]]). The catchment's watershed, biodiversity, and water yield are the primary subject matter for place-based Geography and Life Sciences within the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]].
