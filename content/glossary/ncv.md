A three-year vocational qualification offered at [[tvet-college|TVET colleges]] at [[national-qualifications-framework|NQF]] Levels 2, 3, and 4. Entry is possible from Grade 9. NCV Level 4 is equivalent in standing to the [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] for many post-school entry purposes.
