The [[goe|GoE]] platform's designation for the ~10,000-hectare bioregional area covering the [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]]. Learner biodiversity and heritage data feeds the Valley of Grace Realm through [[bioscore|BioScore]] contributions. In the [[bioregional-learning-centre|BLC]] design, the BLC is the base and the Realm is the campus.
