The primary national legislation governing schools in South Africa, enacted in 1996. Key provisions relevant to this Decision Tool:

- **Section 6** places language-of-instruction decisions at each public school with the [[sgb|School Governing Body]]. The [[wced|WCED]] may not override this (confirmed in the [[mikro-primary-case|Mikro Primary case]]).
- **Section 46** requires independent schools to register with the provincial department; operating an unregistered school is a criminal offence.
