The national policy that governs how public schools accommodate the languages of their learners. It includes the **40-learner threshold**: if 40 learners at a high school request instruction in a particular language, the school must make reasonable effort to accommodate them. In practice, reaching 40 English-medium requests at [[emil-weder-secondary|Emil Weder Secondary]] would require coordinated effort by parents, and the [[sgb|SGB]] retains significant discretion over implementation.
