The spiritual-philosophical framework developed by Rudolf Steiner in the early 20th century, from which [[waldorf-education|Waldorf education]] originates. The relationship between Waldorf pedagogy and anthroposophy varies by school: some foreground it, others treat it as historical background. The Valley of Grace project scope excludes anthroposophical vocabulary from external-facing documents. Parents evaluating a Waldorf school should ask where the school sits on that spectrum.
