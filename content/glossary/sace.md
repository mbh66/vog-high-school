The statutory professional body that registers and regulates teachers in South Africa. A [[caps|CAPS]]-compliant school with [[waldorf-education|Waldorf]] pedagogy needs teachers who are both SACE-registered and trained in Waldorf methods; this is a narrow pool that will be a live constraint for [[silverwood-school|Silverwood]]'s founding cohort.
