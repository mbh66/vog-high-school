A qualification aligned to a specific occupation, accredited by the [[qcto|QCTO]] and typically funded through a [[seta|SETA]]. Distinct from [[caps|CAPS]]-based academic qualifications, occupational certificates combine classroom instruction with workplace practical training. Two examples relevant to valley families:

- Occupational Certificate: Livestock Farmer (NQF 3, OFO 99253), offered by [[content/glossary/elsenburg|Elsenburg]]; entry from Grade 9.
- Occupational Certificate: Orchard and Vineyard Foreman (NQF 4, OFO 110667), offered by Elsenburg; entry from Grade 10 or five years farm supervisory experience.
