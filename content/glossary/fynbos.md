The Cape Floristic Region's characteristic shrubland ecosystem covering the [[riviersonderend|Riviersonderend]] catchment and much of the [[valley-of-grace|Valley of Grace]]. Named as the primary content of Life Sciences in the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|BLC]] curriculum. Fynbos ecology and its management ([[capenature|CapeNature]] partnership, alien vegetation clearing) sit at the intersection of the BLC's education and stewardship functions.
