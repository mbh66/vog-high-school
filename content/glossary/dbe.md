The national department responsible for schooling from Grade R to Grade 12. The DBE mandates [[caps|CAPS]], sets and administers the [[national-senior-certificate|NSC]] for public schools and some independents, and sets policies such as [[quintile|quintile]] classification and the [[language-in-education-policy|Language in Education Policy]].

In the Western Cape, provincial implementation runs through the [[wced|WCED]].
