The Western Cape provincial conservation authority. CapeNature manages the [[greyton|Greyton]] Nature Reserve and, jointly with other bodies, the Riviersonderend Complex under the [[pamp|Protected Area Management Plan]]. Education and awareness are named as management objectives in the Riviersonderend Complex PAMP, which gives the proposed [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]] a formal hook for a conservation partnership.
