The elected body of parents, teachers, and (at high school level) learners that governs a public school under [[sasa|SASA]]. The SGB sets the language policy (see the [[mikro-primary-case|Mikro Primary case]]), approves the school budget and fees, and employs teachers in governing-body-funded posts alongside the [[wced|WCED]]-employed staff. In a public school, the SGB is the parent's formal channel of governance.

Independent schools are governed by a board of directors, a trust, or a Non-Profit Company, not an elected SGB. Parents in independent schools typically have less direct governance authority.
