The citizen-science and stewardship platform that hosts [[bioscore|BioScore]], the [[nature-realm|Nature Realm]] structure, and [[bioquest|BioQuest]] cycles. The valley's proposed education proposals ([[silverwood-school|Silverwood]], the [[bioregional-learning-centre|Bioregional Learning Centre]], the [[valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]]) all treat GoE integration as a structural feature of the curriculum.
