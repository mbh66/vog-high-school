A separate certificate required by South African universities for admission when a learner has completed a non-NSC school-leaving qualification. Cambridge students applying to a South African university apply for it through Universities South Africa (USAf). The process is administrative in nature and adds a step that [[caps|CAPS]] learners do not face.
