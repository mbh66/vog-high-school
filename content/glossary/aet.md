The four-level programme covering basic adult education in literacy, numeracy, and life skills. AET Levels 1 to 3 are pre-GETC; AET Level 4 equates to the [[getc|GETC]] (Grade 9 equivalent). Delivered through public [[cet-college|Community Education and Training colleges]] such as the Overberg CLC and its satellites.
