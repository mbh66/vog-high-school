The Further Education and Training phase of South African schooling: Grades 10, 11, and 12. During the FET phase every learner takes seven subjects: four compulsory (Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy, Life Orientation) and three electives from the [[caps|CAPS]] approved list.

Elective choices made at Grade 10 determine which university programmes and [[tvet-college|TVET]] pathways are open at matric. A school that does not offer Physical Sciences closes the door to medicine and engineering; a school offering only Mathematical Literacy closes the door to any programme requiring Mathematics.

Switching schools or examination bodies during the FET phase is difficult because [[school-based-assessment|SBA]] records may not transfer cleanly.
