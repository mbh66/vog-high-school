# Silverwood-Specific Questions

Silverwood School appears across the other nine variables. This page consolidates everything a parent needs to ask and verify before treating Silverwood as a real option for 2028.

## What is known

The following comes from the Ward 2 Schools Program proposal (July 2026), the Overberg BioHub Founding Brief, and the VoG TGF Project Outline. All three are project documents authored by Michael Haupt or prepared under his coordination. They describe Silverwood in the context of the valley's wider economic and educational architecture.

- Silverwood is described as a CAPS-compliant, Waldorf-inspired high school with project-based learning as the governing pedagogy.
- It is intended to be integrated with the BioHub's BioScore and Nature Realm platforms. Learner work feeds the GoE Valley of Grace Realm.
- The BioHub Founding Brief places it at Genadendal, within the Learning Hub.
- The Ward 2 Schools Program proposal names it alongside Emil Weder Secondary as an initial invitee for the curriculum modules, noting "once its constitution is confirmed."
- The VoG TGF Project Outline lists it as a project partner for citizen science and heritage research.
- The Rotary Club of Greyton has committed R245,000 to the broader TGF project, of which the schools programme is a component.

**Evidence status:** All of the above is MS (Mission-Sourced). None has been independently corroborated by a third party, a WCED filing, or the school's own public communications.

## What is not known

The following questions are unanswered in any document available to the decision tool. Each one is flagged **TBV** (To Be Verified). Parents should seek direct answers from the Silverwood founders before making enrolment decisions.

### Registration and legal standing

1. **Is Silverwood registered with the WCED as an independent school?** If not, when is registration expected? A school that is not registered cannot legally operate (SASA Section 46). A child enrolled in an unregistered school risks having their year not recognised for progression or certification.

2. **Which examination body will Silverwood register with?** IEB, SACAI, or the DBE? This determines which NSC the child writes, how SBAs are moderated, and where the matric exam is written. (See [[Variable 4: Curriculum and Examination Body]].)

3. **What is the school's legal entity?** An NPC, a trust, a Section 21 company? Who are the directors or trustees? What governance rights do parents have?

4. **Has the school obtained Umalusi accreditation (directly or through its examination body)?** An independent school must either be accredited by Umalusi or registered with an examination body that is. Without this, the matric qualification has no standing.

### Curriculum and pedagogy

5. **What does "Waldorf-inspired" mean in practice?** Does Silverwood follow the full 12-year Waldorf curriculum with a dedicated Class 13 matric year, or does it follow CAPS content throughout with Waldorf teaching methods? The distinction affects the length of schooling (12 vs 13 years), the pacing of content, and the transferability of the child's progress if they leave the school mid-stream.

6. **What does "project-based learning as the governing pedagogy" mean for assessment?** Are SBAs conducted through projects, or do conventional tests and exams sit alongside the project work? How are CAPS content requirements met within project structures? How does the school demonstrate to the examination body that content coverage is complete?

7. **Which FET elective subjects will Silverwood offer?** A start-up school with a small initial cohort will not offer a full subject menu. Parents need to know whether their child's required subjects will be available. (See [[Variable 7: Subject Availability]].)

8. **How does the GoE integration work in the classroom?** Is citizen science fieldwork timetabled, or is it extracurricular? Do GoE contributions count toward SBAs, or are they additional? What happens if the GoE platform changes its terms or structure?

### Location and premises

9. **Where will the school be located?** The BioHub Founding Brief places it at Genadendal. The Greyton Commonage Research Report documents a contested R100 sale of ~8 ha on Erf 595 to "a Greyton private school." Are these the same proposal? Has a site been confirmed? What is the fallback if the intended site is unavailable?

10. **Do the premises meet WCED health, safety, and building requirements for a school?** Registration requires this. If the school intends to use an existing building (at Genadendal or elsewhere), has it been assessed?

### Financial viability

11. **What is the fee structure?** Annual tuition, registration fee, any levies, payment terms. Is there a bursary or sliding-scale provision? (See [[Variable 5: Cost]].)

12. **What is the minimum enrolment needed for the school to be financially viable?** If Silverwood needs 40 learners to break even and only 20 enrol, what happens? Do fees increase? Does the school close? Parents need to understand the viability threshold before committing.

13. **What is the financial fallback plan?** If the school fails to reach viable enrolment or runs into financial difficulty mid-year, what protections exist for enrolled learners? Can they transfer their SBA records to another school or provider? Who covers the transition cost?

14. **Is there a founding cohort commitment mechanism?** Some start-up schools ask founding families to commit (financially or through a letter of intent) before opening. Has Silverwood done this? How many families have committed?

### Staffing

15. **Who are the teachers?** A CAPS-compliant school with Waldorf pedagogy needs teachers who are both SACE-registered and trained in Waldorf methods. This is a narrow pool. How many teachers are confirmed, and for which subjects?

16. **Who is the principal or school leader?** Their background, qualifications, and track record in both Waldorf and CAPS environments matter.

### Timeline

17. **Is Silverwood planning to open in January 2028?** If so, WCED registration, premises, staffing, curriculum design, and founding cohort enrolment all need to be complete by mid to late 2027. What is the current status of each?

18. **What is the first intake?** Grade 8 only, or multiple grades simultaneously? If Grade 8 only, the school will not offer Grade 12 (matric) until 2032. Parents enrolling for 2028 are trusting that the school will still exist and be accredited in 2032.

## How to use this page

This page is a checklist, not a verdict. Silverwood may answer every question satisfactorily. It may answer some and leave others open. The purpose of listing the questions is to ensure that parents' enthusiasm for a local school (which is entirely reasonable) does not outpace the evidence about whether this particular school is ready.

A parent who asks these questions at a public meeting or in a one-to-one conversation with the founders is doing due diligence, not expressing hostility. The founders should welcome the questions. If they cannot answer them, that is itself information.

## The collective decision

Silverwood's viability depends on collective action. If enough families commit, the school becomes viable and the valley gains an English-medium, place-based high school option it currently lacks. If too few commit, the school does not open and families must fall back on the other options described in this tool.

The planning session is the place to test that commitment. The tool's role is to ensure that the commitment, if made, is informed.
